The starting point

We get your teams aligned.

Before we touch a single record, we get everyone in the room: Sales, Marketing, Operations, and Finance, whoever owns or depends on your data. We find where definitions break down, where data gets duplicated or lost, and where your teams disagree. Then we fix it together.

  • Align on what "customer" actually means to everyone
  • Map where data lives and who owns it
  • Find and fix the gaps: duplicates, contradictions, and stale records
  • Build trust that the data is right
Engagement / How we approach it
How we approach it
You sign off at every step
  1. Discover what "right" looks likeWorkshops with your teams. What metrics matter? What counts as a customer? Where do definitions clash?
  2. Map and diagnoseWe audit your sources and show you exactly where alignment breaks down. Read-only, no changes yet.
  3. Fix and verifyDeduplicate, normalize, and validate. You sign off at every step, so trust builds as we go.
