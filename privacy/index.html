Introduction

Datahooks ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process your information in connection with our website, services, and business operations.

We align teams around their data, fix data quality issues, and build the dashboards, automations, and AI foundations businesses need. Our services involve processing and analyzing data on behalf of our clients. Your privacy is fundamental to how we operate.

Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information You Provide Directly: When you visit our website, complete contact forms, request services, or communicate with us, we collect information such as your name, company name, job title, email address, phone number, and any messages or details you provide.
  • Information from Your Browser and Devices: We automatically collect certain information about your device when you visit our website, including IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our site, and improve your experience.
  • Client Data: As a data agency, we may process data on behalf of our clients under confidential agreements. This data is handled according to strict contractual obligations and applicable data protection laws.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Responding to inquiries and providing customer support
  • Delivering and improving our services
  • Communicating about service updates, changes, or security notices
  • Analyzing website usage to enhance user experience
  • Conducting business operations and maintaining our services
  • Complying with legal obligations and protecting our rights
  • Marketing our services (only to those who have opted in)

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. However, we may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share information with vendors and service providers who assist us in operating our website and providing services, bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government request.
  • Business Transfers: If Datahooks is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • Client Consent: When processing data on behalf of clients, we only use and share that data according to client instructions and applicable agreements.

Cookies and tracking technologies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and understand how you use our site. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

We use analytics tools to track website performance and user behavior. This information helps us improve our services but does not identify you personally.

Data security

Datahooks takes data security seriously. We implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encryption, secure servers, and limited access controls.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access and review your personal information
  • The right to request correction or deletion of inaccurate data
  • The right to opt out of marketing communications
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to withdraw consent

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through our contact form on the website, and we will respond to your request within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, our privacy practices, or wish to exercise your privacy rights, please reach out to us through our contact form at datahooks.net/contact.

We are committed to working with you to resolve any privacy concerns promptly.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of our website following the posting of changes constitutes your acceptance of those changes.
